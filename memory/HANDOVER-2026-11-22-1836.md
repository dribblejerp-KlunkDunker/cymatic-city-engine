## Key Decisions Made
- **`ENGINE_VERSION` is 1.7.0 from 7a** (sleeves said "bump it", 2026-11-22).
  - Context: CLAUDE.md says contract changes bump the version; the spec had held 1.7.0 for 7e.
  - Rationale: the invariant wins once sleeves chose. Later Phase 7 stages that change the contract bump the patch
    (1.7.1, …) so the minor number keeps naming the phase. Golden hashes normalise the version, so the bump moves
    nothing else. The same push carried the two Codex P2 fixes on PR #4 (empty `underworld` neutral; `ward` in the
    `query_city` schema's `fail.kind`).
- **`pressure` divides the gravity the head rules see** (`headG = g·k_g / k_p`): pressure 2 needs twice the head
  (70 m tanks), 0.5 half. Kept one function so tank height, head limits, PRVs, node bar and `flows()` agree.
- **Partial wards make the water builder zoned** like terrain: tanks sized by the neediest customer of their zone, and
  each starved zone gets one new tank per round. Without this, partial g 0.3 or pressure 0.3 wards failed
  `pressure-zones` (2,599 and 1,470 nodes out). With it every tried case passes; pressure 0.3 over one polarity costs
  284 zones and 791 PRVs, which is the honest consequence of mixing heads that far apart.
- **A ward too light for any tank (needs more than 80 m) is pumped**, as the world is at that gravity: its minimum head
  is not checked, `water-pressure` warns `+ pumps`. Same rule the world follows on a flat plate.
- **`"all"` cannot share the plan with another ward** (refused by name) rather than letting later wards override it.
- **`where.at` is `[x, z]` on flat plates, `[x, y, z]` on a sphere**: the spec only said `[x, z]`; a sphere needs 3D.
- **`magic.ley` / `magic.underworld` truthy are refused** as "not built in engine 1.6.0" until 7b–7d, so a profile
  never silently ignores magic; `false`/`null` stay neutral.
