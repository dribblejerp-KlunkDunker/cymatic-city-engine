## Key Decisions Made
- **Frames stay byte-identical to keyframes; the horizon is recorded in the exchange files, not in `meta`.**
  - **Context**: a `--year` export to `.json` should name its horizon.
  - **Rationale**: the spec's proof requires `cityAt` to equal the keyframe field for field; adding
    `meta.horizon` changes `simulate()` output and the golden hashes. Left for the review to decide.
- **`cityAt` refuses horizons under 5** instead of clamping like `simulate()`.
  - **Rationale**: a silent clamp would return a different city than the one asked for.
- **Water is the plate outline, not a quad; roads get a material per kind; trees are instanced too.**
  - **Rationale**: a quad shows water outside a circular rim; per-kind colours match the page.
- **GeoJSON metric proof is 0.5 % per footprint, 0.1 % over the town.**
  - **Rationale**: 7 decimals of a degree ≈ 1 cm, which is 0.1–0.2 % of a ten-metre house's area.
- **Viewer keeps one Save button** (zip gains files, one origin field) rather than a new export menu.
- **`--year` on all city commands**, because a frame is a complete city and `cityOf(frame.layout)` just works.

## Pitfalls & Gotchas
- `ENGINE_VERSION` is inside `toJSON` (`meta.engine`), so golden hashes normalise it to 1.5.0 before hashing.
- `build.mjs` strips single-line `import` statements only; keep imports in `src/export.js` on one line.
- A glTF `.gltf` names its `.bin` by file name, so two exports with different names differ in one string.
- `Math.min(...array)` overflows the stack on city-sized arrays; use `reduce`.
- `gltf-validator` reports instancing accessors as unused (info level): it does not read the extension.
- Blender expands `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing` into objects (1,180 for the instanced town).
- In this cloud session, building `cjval` with cargo and running a downloaded Godot binary were both blocked.
- The container restarted once mid-session; uncommitted edits survived, background jobs did not.
- The shallow clone tracks only `main`; tracking refs for both branches were added by hand.
