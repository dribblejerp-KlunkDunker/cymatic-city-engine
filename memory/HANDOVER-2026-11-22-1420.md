## Key Decisions Made
- **Wards reach hydraulics, not only structure.**
  - **Context**: the material limit binds on 0 buildings in every stock preset.
  - **Rationale**: half gravity turns 26 lift stations into 134 and doubles tank height; that is where a ward
    is felt outside megastructures. Hence also the recommended `height` multiplier (call W).
- **A street node's ward is its buildings' majority.** Streets are nodal lines, so nodes have no district.
- **Nexuses come from the field, not from landmarks.** The megastructure has no landmarks.
- **Ley = sources and straight lines; street transmission stays.** The station is built only for the
  shortfall. At 600 W/m² the town is 5.1× supplied, the default city 1.39×, the megastructure 0.16×.
- **One pairwise `layer-clearance` check over a registry**, run on today's layers first: anything it finds
  there is a finding, not something to tune away.
- **Deeps follow the octave below (f/2 nodal lines)** and are reached by shafts on street edges, because
  stations are 182–485 m from the nearest deep road.

## Pitfalls & Gotchas
- `fieldPreview().at(u, v)` takes normalised coordinates (`toNorm([x, 0, z])`) and returns `{ S, A }`, not a
  number. S is signed (streets are S = 0); A is the envelope.
- Districts are sign regions: 14 at 432 Hz but 84 antinodes. Do not equate them.
- The measurement scripts use platform `Math` and coarse grids: prototypes. Engine code must use `DM.*`.
- The terrace rule in arcologies is non-local, so "a ward changes only its own district" is false for
  megastructures; the 7a proof says so.
- Nodal lengths in the spec are grid-crossing estimates (about ±10 %).
- The fantasy theme already calls power feeders "Ley lines"; 7e renames them.
