## Key Decisions Made
- **Year needs a horizon; `horizon` joins the profile (default 100).**
  - **Context**: `--year 80` in the roadmap.
  - **Rationale**: growth is scheduled to complete at the horizon and the random draws depend on it, so the
    same year under two horizons is two cities. K (keyframe count) does not matter: measured identical.
- **glTF: per-building nodes sharing meshes by default; `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing` behind `--instanced`.**
  - **Rationale**: Godot's importer lacks the extension (PR #112866 open, milestone 4.8); Blender has it.
- **GeoJSON: exact tangent-plane projection, origin required.**
  - **Rationale**: 2 mm error at 6 km against 5.3 m for the first-order formula at 60° latitude.
- **CityJSON: local coordinates, no CRS, no networks.**
  - **Rationale**: no clean 3D EPSG code for an arbitrary origin; GeoJSON covers GIS placement.
- **Validators**: `gltf-validator` as a pinned devDependency (ran here); `cjval` only at the phase review.
- **MCP page of 25 rows lives in the MCP layer, not in `agentTools()`.**
  - **Rationale**: no engine contract change; the tool's own ceiling of 200 still applies with `limit`.
